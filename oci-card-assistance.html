Are you a foreign citizen of Indian origin seeking an Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) Card?

RRC Law Associates provides legal and documentation assistance to eligible applicants seeking OCI registration, particularly where establishing Indian ancestry, tracing historical family records or resolving documentary discrepancies presents a challenge.

An OCI application may require more than completing an online form. Depending on circumstances, it may be necessary to examine citizenship history, establish multi-generational family links, locate historical records, and address documentary gaps before submission.

Establishing Your Indian Ancestry for an OCI Application

For some applicants, the principal challenge is not the OCI application itself but proving the family connection to India. You may be a foreign citizen whose parents, grandparents or great-grandparents lived in India. However, old family documents may be incomplete, names may have changed over generations, and historical birth or marriage records may be difficult to locate.

Our objective is to help applicants establish a clear, consistent and verifiable documentary connection with their Indian ancestry, where the applicable statutory requirements can be satisfied.

RELEVANT RECORDS TO EXAMINE
  • Birth & marriage certificates across successive generations
  • Old Indian passports & citizenship certificates
  • School records, family registers & educational certificates
  • Historical land records & revenue documentation
  • Death certificates establishing relationships between generations
  • Records available through relevant registration authorities or archives

WHO BENEFITS

Who Should Contact RRC?

  • Foreign citizens whose parents or grandparents were connected with India.
  • Applicants whose Indian-origin family records are incomplete or scattered.
  • Families trying to establish a connection to an ancestor who lived in India many decades ago.
  • Applicants facing difficulties obtaining old birth or marriage records (including NABC cases).
  • Applicants with spelling inconsistencies in names, dates of birth or family relationships.
  • Former Indian citizens who need assistance understanding passport surrender and renunciation records.
  • Foreign spouses and eligible children who need guidance on applicable OCI requirements.
