Resolving Commercial Conflicts and Contractual Breaches

When commercial relationships break down, swift and legally disciplined action is critical to prevent asset dissipation or unrecoverable financial losses.

  • Statutory Legal Demand Notices: Formally placing defaulting commercial counterparties on notice under Indian contract law, establishing unambiguous evidentiary records, and asserting specific performance or liquidated damages.
  • Commercial Courts Act 2015 Proceedings: Instituting fast-track commercial suits before specialized Commercial Courts in Chennai, Thanjavur, and across Tamil Nadu, adhering to strict statutory timelines and mandatory pre-institution mediation.
  • Emergency Section 9 Injunctions: Seeking urgent interim relief from civil courts or Section 17 arbitral tribunals to restrain local partners from siphoning company assets, dissipating bank funds, or creating third-party rights.

Document Checklist

Essential Documents for Commercial Review

To initiate a comprehensive legal assessment of your business contract, transaction, or commercial dispute, please compile the following records.

Contractual & Corporate Records

  • Executed Agreements / Deeds: Partnership deed, LLP agreement, shareholder agreement (SHA), joint venture agreement, or commercial lease.
  • Entity Registration Certificates: Certificate of Incorporation (COI), Form C (Registrar of Firms), or LLP incorporation documents.
  • Constitutional Documents: Memorandum and Articles of Association (MoA & AoA), partnership deeds, and registered amendments.
  • Board & Partner Resolutions: Authorizations, minutes of meetings, or partner consent letters related to the disputed transaction.
  • Commercial Correspondence: Formal notices, email chains, payment reminders, or WhatsApp records evidencing default or breach.

Financial & Evidentiary Records

  • Bank Statements & Wire Proofs: Official banking records showing capital infusion, loans, or contractual disbursements (NRE/NRO/FCNR accounts).
  • Invoices & Delivery Receipts: Commercial purchase orders, GST invoices, bills of lading, or signed delivery receipts.
  • Asset Records: Title deeds, lease deeds, or encumbrance certificates for commercial properties or business equipment.
  • Prior Legal Notices: Demand letters or legal notices issued or received by either party, along with postal acknowledgment cards.
  • Special Power of Attorney (PoA): Consular-attested or apostilled PoA appointing your legal representative in India.
